Our Philosophy

A Digital Sanctuary for Unspoken Things

UNSPOKEN was designed as a quiet sanctuary away from the noise, performance, and judgment of modern social media.

Every day, millions of people walk around carrying heavy feelings they cannot confess to their friends, families, or coworkers. UNSPOKEN gives those words a safe, anonymous place to land.

Absolute Privacy: By default, all personal letters and journal entries are kept strictly in your private local space. If you choose to share anonymously with our community, your letter is gently reviewed to ensure our sanctuary remains free of cruelty, harassment, and spam.

Community Care: We are not licensed medical professionals. If you are experiencing an immediate mental health crisis, please reach out to trusted friends, counselors, or 24/7 crisis hotlines.

Backend Architecture

Supabase Shared Database Integration:
UNSPOKEN is engineered to run seamlessly on GitHub Pages with zero build step, using the official Supabase JavaScript client.

Security & Row Level Security (RLS):
Anonymous visitors have insert-only access for pending content. Only authenticated administrators with authorized roles can approve public submissions. Anonymous visitors cannot approve content or change moderation flags.

Configuring Supabase:
Open script.js and update SUPABASE_URL and SUPABASE_ANON_KEY with your project credentials. Run the provided SQL setup script in your Supabase SQL editor.
